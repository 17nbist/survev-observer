survev observer

A small script for Tampermonkey that records what your survev game already shows you while you play: where the players on your screen are, where they aim, and their shots. It sends that to the clip jury, which uses it to find players whose aim looks inhuman.

It does not change your game. No aim help, no wallhack, no automation. It only reads.

Install (Tampermonkey) Read the code

What it sends: positions, facing, weapons and gear of players in view, bullets, kills, obstacles and smoke, all by the game's own player numbers. Never names, your account, or anything from your browser. Your IP address is not stored, only a scrambled version used to stop spam.

Turning it off: click the small "observer" label in the bottom left corner of the game to pause it, or disable the script in Tampermonkey.
